# Healthify Pro

Build a premium fitness app called "Healthify" — 

the ultimate all-in-one health platform combining 

AI coaching, gamification, social features, and 

comprehensive health tracking.

COLOR PALETTE:

- Primary: #6C63FF (Electric Purple)

- Secondary: #00D4AA (Mint Green)  

- Accent: #FF6B6B (Coral Red)

- Background: #0A0A0F (Deep Black)

- Card: #12121A (Dark Card)

- Text: #FFFFFF and #94A3B8

TYPOGRAPHY:

- Headers: Bold, large, impactful

- Body: Clean, readable

- Numbers: Monospace for stats

DESIGN STYLE:

- Dark premium UI like Nike Training Club

- Glassmorphism cards

- Smooth gradients

- Micro-animations on interactions

- Bottom tab navigation

- Floating action buttons

===== SCREENS TO BUILD =====

1. ONBOARDING FLOW

- Welcome screen with app logo and tagline

  "Your AI-Powered Fitness Universe"

- Step 1: Basic Info (Age, Height, Weight, Gender)

- Step 2: Goal Selection (Fat Loss / Muscle Gain / 

  Endurance / General Fitness) with visual cards

- Step 3: Activity Level (Sedentary/Light/Moderate/

  Active/Athlete) with animated icons

- Step 4: Diet Preference (Veg/Non-Veg/Vegan/Keto/

  Indian) with food imagery

- Step 5: Gym Experience (Beginner/Intermediate/

  Advanced) with skill indicators

- Step 6: Lifestyle (Sleep hours, Stress level, 

  Workout time preference, Medical conditions)

- Step 7: AI Plan Generation screen with loading 

  animation showing "AI generating your personalized

  plan..." with progress steps

- Step 8: Plan Ready screen showing 

  Daily Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat, 

  Water Goal, Workout Split

2. HOME DASHBOARD

- Top: "Good Morning, [Name]! 💪" greeting

- Today's Ring Progress (Calories/Protein/Water/

  Workout) like Apple Watch rings

- Quick Stats Cards:

  * Calories: Consumed vs Goal

  * Protein: Grams left

  * Water: Glasses remaining

  * Sleep Score from last night

- Today's Workout Card (Start button, 

  muscle groups, estimated time)

- AI Coach Tip of the day (glassmorphism card)

- Recovery Score with color indicator

- Mood Check-in buttons (5 emoji options)

- Active Streak counter with fire animation

- Today's Challenges (3 mini missions)

- Weight Trend mini graph (7 days)

- Bottom Navigation: Home, Workout, 

  Nutrition, Progress, Profile

3. WORKOUT MODULE

- Workout Home: Today's plan + Templates + History

- Exercise Library with search and muscle filter

- Exercise Detail page:

  * Exercise name and muscle groups targeted

  * 3D Muscle diagram showing activated muscles

    (front and back body view highlighted)

  * Sets/Reps/Weight logger

  * Rest Timer (circular countdown)

  * RPE slider (1-10)

  * Tempo input (e.g. 3-1-2)

  * Notes field

  * PR badge if new personal record

- Active Workout Screen:

  * Current exercise with set tracker

  * Superset builder toggle

  * Dropset mode

  * Warm-up sets (marked separately)

  * Rest timer popup

  * Muscle Heatmap showing worked muscles

  * Estimated 1RM calculation

  * Workout Score (live)

- Post Workout Summary:

  * Total volume lifted

  * Personal records broken

  * Muscle groups worked (heatmap)

  * Workout Score with rating

  * XP earned animation

  * Share to social button

4. NUTRITION MODULE

- Nutrition Home: 

  * Calorie ring (consumed vs goal)

  * Macro bars (Protein/Carbs/Fat)

  * Meal sections (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snacks)

  * Each meal shows calories and macros

- Add Food options:

  * Search bar with instant results

  * Barcode Scanner button (camera icon)

  * Meal Photo AI button (camera with AI badge)

  * Voice logging button (microphone)

  * Recent foods

  * My recipes

- Food Detail page:

  * Full macro breakdown

  * Micronutrients (Fiber, Sugar, Sodium, 

    Vitamins, Minerals)

  * Serving size adjuster

  * Add to meal button

- Indian Foods section with popular dishes

- Recipe Creator with ingredient builder

- Weekly Nutrition Report with graphs

- Meal timing recommendations

5. PROGRESS MODULE

- Progress Home with tabs:

  Weight | Body | Strength | Photos

- Weight Graph (interactive, zoom-able)

- Body Measurements tracker 

  (Chest, Waist, Hips, Arms, Thighs)

- Body Fat % tracker with visual body silhouette

- Stats Cards: BMI, FFMI, Lean Body Mass

- Progress Photos: 

  Before/After comparison slider

  Calendar view with photo thumbnails

- Strength Progress graphs per exercise

- Muscle Gain Timeline

- Calories vs Weight correlation graph

- Protein vs Muscle Gain graph

6. AI COACH SCREEN

- Chat interface with AI Coach

- AI avatar (animated, friendly robot/coach)

- Quick Action buttons:

  * "Analyze my workout"

  * "Review my diet today"

  * "Give me motivation"

  * "Home workout alternative"

  * "Weekly review"

- Chat bubbles with AI responses

- Daily Motivation card at top

- Weekly Review summary card

- Monthly Progress Report button

7. RECOVERY & WELLNESS

- Recovery Score (large circular gauge, 0-100)

- Sleep Tracking:

  * Hours slept

- Stress Level slider

- Energy Level indicator

- Mood tracker with emoji

  * Large visual water bottle filling up

  * Glass count (e.g. 6/8 glasses)

  * Add water button (+250ml quick add)

  * Reminder settings

8. GAMIFICATION HUB

- Player Profile Card:

  * Avatar (unlockable)

  * Level (e.g. Level 12 - Iron Warrior)

  * XP bar showing progress to next level

- Active Streaks:

  * Workout Streak 🔥

- Weekly Missions (bigger rewards)

- Badges Collection grid

- Unlockable Themes preview

- Feed with friend activity:

  * Workout completions

  * Progress photo shares

  * Badge unlocks

- Challenge friends button

- Find Friends search

- My Posts tab

- Profile header with avatar, name, level badge

- Stats overview: 

  Total Workouts, Total Volume, Streak Record
